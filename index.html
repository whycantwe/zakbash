TWENTY-FOUR YEARS BEHIND THE KIT.

Zak Glosserman started early. In 2002 he co-founded Orange in Los Angeles with singer-bassist Joe Dexter, the two of them barely into their teens, alongside guitarists Brendan Minded and Perry Ladish.

A friend passed their demo to Tim Armstrong of Rancid, and Hellcat Records signed the band while they were still in high school. Geffen and Sony had come calling too. They went with the punk label and spent years growing into it.

What followed was twelve years and four records — Welcome to the World of…, Escape from L.A., Phoenix and the Dead Sexy EP — four straight Give ’Em the Boot compilations, a Cartoon Network theme, and most of the punk touring circuit: Rancid, the Misfits, the UK Subs, the Adicts, the Warped Tour. He left in 2014.

He didn’t stop drumming. A single with Coyote Shivers in 2015, the recordings that became Ingrata’s Demo ’24, and now Cockring — a Sacramento hardcore punk band four releases deep, with records out on No Time.

THE ORANGE YEARS.

In 2002, Zak and vocalist-bassist Joe Dexter started Orange in Los Angeles while still in their early teens. By sixteen they had a deal with Hellcat, and by 2005 a debut album out on it.

He drummed for the band for twelve years — through four records, a run of television placements, and most of the punk world’s touring circuit — before stepping away in 2014.
